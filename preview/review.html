Take a look around

To watch the opening, scroll down on signup and tap Play closet opening. The doors open, the clothing rail moves forward, and the hangers become the Mikloset logo. Use Replay to watch again or the back arrow to return to the demo.

On the app's signup screen, scroll down and choose Preview the mobile app. You do not need to sign up or enter credentials.

To review Google signup, choose Continue with Google, select Demo user, complete the sample profile and verify with code 123456. After the demo agreement, an empty dashboard offers Connect Now or Later for Gmail. No real Google account is accessed.

For the non-Gmail journey, choose Sign up with email, use sample details and a made-up password, then enter the sample email code 123456. Continue with manual add or assisted receipt/store options.

Explore Home, Closet, Add, Style and Looks. Use the Home avatar for profile and settings.

For populated styling, builder and account examples, open Screen walkthrough.

Phone verification, Google sign-in and Gmail discovery are simulated. No SMS is sent. Discovered sample items stay pending until you approve them. Styling requires 10 closet items.
